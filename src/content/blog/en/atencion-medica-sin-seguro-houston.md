Not having health insurance does not mean going without care. Houston has several options, and knowing them helps you spend less and avoid waiting until a small problem becomes an emergency. Here they are, from fastest to most paperwork.

## 1. A self-pay clinic

For a visit, lab work or a routine exam, a clinic that sees uninsured patients is usually fastest: you walk in, ask the price and are seen the same day. Always ask for the cost **before** any test is done.

## 2. Your right to a good faith estimate

If you pay without insurance, the federal No Surprises Act gives you the right to ask for a written **good faith estimate**. If the visit is scheduled at least 3 business days ahead, the provider must give you one. If the final bill is **$400 or more** above the estimate, you can dispute it.

## 3. Community health centers

Federally funded health centers charge based on income (sliding scale) and see patients regardless of immigration status or insurance. You can find the nearest one with HRSA's official locator.

## 4. County financial assistance

Harris Health has a financial assistance program for Harris County residents who meet income requirements. It gives access to its clinics and hospitals at reduced cost.

## 5. Marketplace insurance

If your immigration status allows it, you can buy a plan at **HealthCare.gov**. Many families qualify for subsidies that lower the premium a lot. Open enrollment usually runs **November 1 to January 15**; outside those dates you need a qualifying event, such as losing your job or having a baby.

## 6. Medicaid and CHIP for children

In Texas, many children qualify for Medicaid or CHIP even if their parents do not. It is worth applying at **YourTexasBenefits.com**.

## What if it is an emergency?

Chest pain, trouble breathing, bleeding that will not stop or signs of stroke are for the emergency room or **911**. By law, hospitals with emergency departments must evaluate and stabilize you even if you have no insurance or cannot pay at that moment.

## How to spend less

- Ask about **packages or promotions** for checkups.
- Ask for the **generic medication** and compare pharmacy prices.
- Do not let an infection linger: early treatment costs less.
