If this is your first time at Clínica Hispana Mangum, this guide walks you through the visit from start to finish so you arrive with no questions. We are a family medical clinic in northwest Houston, with care in Spanish and English.

## Where are we and when are we open?

- **Address:** 2912 Mangum Rd, Suite A, Houston, TX 77092.
- **Hours:** every day, Monday through Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- **Parking:** free, in front of the clinic, with accessible spaces.
- **Accessibility:** wheelchair-accessible entrance and restrooms.

## Do I need an appointment?

No. You can come any time during open hours and we see patients in order of arrival. If you prefer, call first to ask about the wait or to let us know you are on your way.

## What happens at the first visit?

1. **Check-in:** we ask for your name, date of birth and a phone number.
2. **Vital signs:** blood pressure, temperature, weight and pulse.
3. **Visit:** the clinic's medical team listens to what is going on, examines you and explains in your language.
4. **Tests or treatment:** if you need lab work, tests or wound care, many can be done during the same visit.
5. **Instructions:** you leave knowing what to do, when to come back and when to go to the emergency room.

## What should I bring?

- A photo ID, if you have one.
- A list of medications and allergies.
- Previous results or tests related to your visit.
- Vaccination records if you are coming for a school or immigration exam.

## How do I pay?

You do not need health insurance. Ask the price before the visit or test; we accept **cash, cards and Apple Pay**. We also have [current promotions](/en/promociones) for checkups.

## What do we treat?

Family medicine and [chronic disease care](/en/services/condiciones-cronicas), [blood tests](/en/services/examenes-sangre), [gynecology](/en/services/ginecologia), [men's health](/en/services/salud-hombre), [DOT](/en/services/examen-dot), [I-693 immigration](/en/services/examenes-inmigracion) and school exams, vaccines, wound care and minor surgery. See the [full list of services](/en/services).

## How can I reach you?

Call **(832) 834-5507** or send a WhatsApp message to the same number. You can also find us on Facebook and Instagram as clinicahispanamangum. If you have an emergency, such as chest pain or trouble breathing, call **911**.
